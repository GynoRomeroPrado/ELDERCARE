# ELDERCARE+ Medicare Reimbursement Strategy

## Executive Summary

ELDERCARE+ is positioned to capture Medicare reimbursement through **Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM)** and **Chronic Care Management (CCM)** programs. This strategy outlines the pathway to become a reimbursable medical service, enabling healthcare providers to bill Medicare for monitoring elderly patients with the ELDERCARE+ platform.

**Revenue Opportunity:**
- **Per Patient/Month:** $150-200 in Medicare reimbursement
- **Provider Revenue Share:** 30% to ELDERCARE+, 70% to provider
- **Target:** 10,000 patients by Year 2 = $45-60M annual revenue (provider side)

## Medicare Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) Overview

### What is RPM?

Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) allows healthcare providers to monitor patients outside of traditional clinical settings using connected medical devices. Medicare covers RPM services for beneficiaries with chronic conditions.

**Key Benefits:**
- Reduces hospital readmissions (20-30% reduction)
- Improves medication adherence (40% improvement)
- Early detection of health deterioration
- Reduces emergency department visits
- Improves patient satisfaction and outcomes

### RPM Eligibility

**Patient Eligibility:**
- Medicare Part B beneficiary
- Has one or more chronic conditions expected to last ≥3 months
  - Common conditions: Heart failure, diabetes, COPD, hypertension, falls
- Patient provides verbal consent for RPM services
- Patient is not in hospice care

**Provider Eligibility:**
- Physician (MD, DO)
- Nurse Practitioner (NP)
- Physician Assistant (PA)
- Clinical Nurse Specialist (CNS)
- Certified Nurse Midwife (CNM)
- Must be enrolled in Medicare

## RPM CPT Codes and Reimbursement

### CPT Code Breakdown

#### 1. CPT 99453: Device Setup and Patient Education
**Description:** Initial setup and patient education on use of RPM device(s)
**Requirements:**
- One-time per episode of care
- Device given to patient
- Patient educated on proper use
- Can be performed by clinical staff under supervision

**Reimbursement:** $19.46 (2024 Medicare rate, non-facility)
**Time Required:** N/A (one-time setup)

**ELDERCARE+ Application:**
- Installation of fall detection sensor
- Setup of smart pill dispenser
- Mobile app installation and training
- Family caregiver onboarding

---

#### 2. CPT 99454: Device Supply with Data Transmission
**Description:** Supply of device and ≥16 days of data collection/transmission per 30-day period
**Requirements:**
- Device must be FDA-cleared (Class II medical device)
- Automatic data collection and transmission
- Minimum 16 days of data in a 30-day period
- Physiologic data (not self-reported)

**Reimbursement:** $64.12 (2024 Medicare rate, non-facility)
**Time Required:** N/A (automated data collection)
**Billing Frequency:** Monthly (if 16+ days met)

**ELDERCARE+ Application:**
- Fall detection events (date, time, severity)
- Medication adherence data (taken/missed doses)
- Activity patterns (movement detected by sensors)
- Environmental data (temperature, humidity for context)
- Device connectivity/uptime logs

**Data Requirements:**
- Minimum 16 days per month with ≥1 data point
- Data automatically transmitted to provider portal
- Provider has access to review data

---

#### 3. CPT 99457: First 20 Minutes of RPM Services
**Description:** Remote physiologic monitoring treatment management services, first 20 minutes of clinical staff/physician time per calendar month
**Requirements:**
- Requires ≥20 minutes of interactive communication with patient
- Must be provided by physician or qualified healthcare professional
- Can be split across multiple days in the month
- Must be related to RPM data review and management

**Reimbursement:** $50.72 (2024 Medicare rate, non-facility)
**Time Required:** 20 minutes
**Billing Frequency:** Once per month

**ELDERCARE+ Application:**
**Qualified Activities (20-minute requirement):**
- Review fall detection events and trends
- Discuss medication adherence issues
- Modify medication schedule based on adherence data
- Assess fall risk based on activity patterns
- Counsel patient on fall prevention strategies
- Review alerts and caregiver responses
- Coordinate care with family members
- Adjust monitoring parameters
- Document clinical decision-making

**How ELDERCARE+ Supports This:**
- Provider dashboard with data summaries
- Automated monthly reports (save provider time)
- Alert prioritization (focus on high-risk patients)
- Pre-populated clinical notes templates
- Integrated video call for patient interaction (via telemedicine)

---

#### 4. CPT 99458: Additional 20 Minutes of RPM Services
**Description:** Each additional 20 minutes of clinical staff/physician time beyond initial 20 minutes
**Requirements:**
- Same as 99457
- Can be billed multiple times per month (rare in practice)

**Reimbursement:** $40.50 (2024 Medicare rate, non-facility)
**Time Required:** 20 minutes (additional)
**Billing Frequency:** As needed (typically 0-1 times per month)

**ELDERCARE+ Application:**
- Complex patients requiring more time
- Recent fall event requiring extensive follow-up
- Medication non-compliance intervention
- Care coordination with specialists

---

#### 5. CPT 99091: Collection and Interpretation of Physiologic Data (Alternative)
**Description:** Collection and interpretation of physiologic data stored and transmitted by patient
**Requirements:**
- ≥30 minutes of time spent reviewing data
- Data review and interpretation by physician/QHP
- Medical decision-making based on data

**Reimbursement:** $56.66 (2024 Medicare rate, non-facility)
**Time Required:** ≥30 minutes
**Billing Frequency:** Once per month

**Note:** Typically use 99457/99458 instead, as they're more straightforward for RPM

## Chronic Care Management (CCM) - Complementary Revenue

### CCM Overview

Chronic Care Management (CCM) services support patients with ≥2 chronic conditions expected to last ≥12 months. CCM focuses on comprehensive care coordination.

**CCM can be billed alongside RPM** - they're complementary programs.

### CCM CPT Codes

#### CPT 99490: CCM - First 20 Minutes
**Description:** Chronic care management services, first 20 minutes per calendar month
**Requirements:**
- ≥2 chronic conditions expected to last ≥12 months
- 24/7 access to care team
- Comprehensive care plan
- Medication reconciliation
- ≥20 minutes of non-face-to-face time

**Reimbursement:** $42.60 (2024 Medicare rate)

**ELDERCARE+ Support:**
- Family chat for 24/7 caregiver communication
- Medication list management in app
- Care plan documentation
- Task coordination among care team

#### CPT 99439: CCM - Additional 20 Minutes
**Reimbursement:** $37.31 per additional 20 minutes

### Combined RPM + CCM Revenue

CPT 99490 (CCM 20 min, monthly):     $42.60

## ELDERCARE+ Provider Revenue Model

### Revenue Sharing Model

**Option 1: Platform Fee Model**
- Provider bills Medicare, receives reimbursement
- Provider pays ELDERCARE+ platform fee
  - $50/patient/month for software + hardware lease
- Provider keeps remainder (~$107/patient/month)

**Option 2: Revenue Share Model**
- ELDERCARE+ bills on behalf of provider (if licensed)
- Revenue split: 30% ELDERCARE+, 70% provider
  - ELDERCARE+: ~$47/patient/month
  - Provider: ~$110/patient/month

**Option 3: Direct-to-Consumer + Provider Billing**
- Patient pays $29.99/month for monitoring service (B2C)
- Provider bills Medicare for clinical services
- ELDERCARE+ doesn't need to split Medicare revenue
- Provider uses free portal (no platform fee)

### Provider Value Proposition

**For Primary Care Physicians:**
- **New Revenue Stream:** $100-150 per patient/month
  - 100 patients = $10,000-15,000/month = $120-180K/year
- **Improved Outcomes:** Reduce readmissions, ER visits
- **Time Efficiency:** Automated data collection saves nursing time
- **Patient Satisfaction:** Better care, more engagement
- **Competitive Advantage:** Offer modern, tech-enabled care

**For Geriatricians:**
- Ideal patient population (elderly with multiple chronic conditions)
- RPM + CCM both applicable
- Fall prevention is billable clinical service
- Medication management is core competency

**For Home Health Agencies:**
- Supplement in-home visits with continuous monitoring
- Early detection of deterioration
- Better care coordination with physicians

### Step 3: Billing Infrastructure

**Requirements:**

**1. Provider Enrollment:**
- Must be enrolled in Medicare
- NPI (National Provider Identifier) number
- PTAN (Provider Transaction Access Number)

**2. Documentation Requirements:**
- **Patient Consent:** Verbal consent documented in chart
  - "Patient [Name] consents to RPM services using ELDERCARE+ fall detection and medication monitoring system"
- **Care Plan:** Documented care plan including RPM
- **Time Tracking:** Document time spent on 99457/99458
  - ELDERCARE+ auto-generates time logs
- **Clinical Notes:** Document review of RPM data and clinical decisions
  - Template: "Reviewed ELDERCARE+ data for [Date Range]. Patient had [X] fall alerts, [Y]% medication adherence..."

**3. ELDERCARE+ Billing Support:**
- **Provider Portal Features:**
  - Billable patient roster
  - Compliance tracking (16+ days data)
  - Time tracking for 99457/99458
  - Pre-filled billing templates
  - Monthly billing summary
  - Export to practice management systems

---

### Step 4: Medicare Advantage Integration

**Opportunity:** Medicare Advantage (MA) plans cover 50%+ of Medicare beneficiaries

**MA Plan Benefits:**
- Can cover additional services beyond traditional Medicare
- Risk-based contracts incentivize preventing adverse events
- Faster payment cycles than traditional Medicare
- Can negotiate supplemental benefits (e.g., free devices for members)

**Partnership Model:**
- Contract directly with MA plans (e.g., Humana, UnitedHealthcare)
- ELDERCARE+ as in-network RPM provider
- Per-member-per-month (PMPM) capitation
  - Target: $60 PMPM for monitoring services
  - Plan saves money via reduced hospitalization costs

**Target Plans:**
1. Humana (5.8M Medicare Advantage members)
2. UnitedHealthcare (6.8M MA members)
3. Blue Cross Blue Shield plans
4. Regional MA plans (easier entry)

---

### Step 5: Quality Reporting

**Medicare Quality Programs:**

**MIPS (Merit-based Incentive Payment System):**
- Providers can earn bonus payments for quality outcomes
- RPM-relevant measures:
  - Falls screening (universal for 65+)
  - Medication reconciliation post-discharge
  - Advance care planning
  - Patient engagement (patient portal use)

**ELDERCARE+ Support:**
- Auto-generate quality measure reports
- Document falls screening in provider notes
- Track medication adherence (MIPS measure)

## Risks and Mitigation

### Regulatory Risks

**Risk 1: FDA Clearance Delay**
- **Impact:** Cannot bill Medicare until FDA-cleared
- **Mitigation:**
  - Pre-submission meeting with FDA (de-risk pathway)
  - Start B2C business first (non-medical claims)
  - Prepare provider network in parallel

**Risk 2: Medicare Coverage Policy Change**
- **Impact:** CPT codes could be eliminated or reimbursement reduced
- **Mitigation:**
  - Diversify: B2C + provider + MA plans
  - Demonstrate clinical outcomes (support for coverage)
  - Engage in policy advocacy (via trade associations)

### Commercial Risks

**Risk 3: Provider Adoption**
- **Impact:** Providers don't sign up or don't bill Medicare
- **Mitigation:**
  - Turnkey billing support (make it easy)
  - Free pilot program (prove value)
  - Revenue guarantee (we'll help them get ROI)

**Risk 4: Patient Compliance**
- **Impact:** <16 days of data = no billing
- **Mitigation:**
  - User-friendly setup
  - Automated reminders (device connectivity)
  - Family engagement (encourage elder to use system)

**Risk 5: Reimbursement Denials**
- **Impact:** Medicare denies claims, provider loses revenue
- **Mitigation:**
  - Documentation templates (ensure compliant)
  - Real-time billing compliance checks (portal feature)
  - Appeal support (dedicated team)
