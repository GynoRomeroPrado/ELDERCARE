# ELDERCARE+ Go-to-Market Strategy

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the comprehensive go-to-market (GTM) strategy for ELDERCARE+, targeting a $30B market opportunity in aging-in-place technology. Our multi-channel approach focuses on direct-to-consumer sales, healthcare provider partnerships, and senior living facility contracts.

## Market Segmentation

### Primary Market Segments

**1. Direct-to-Consumer (B2C)**
- **Target**: Families with aging parents (age 65+)
- **Size**: 53M family caregivers in USA
- **Profile**:
  - Age 45-65 (adult children caring for parents)
  - Household income >$75K
  - Tech-savvy, concerned about parent's safety
  - Geographically distributed from parents
- **Pain Points**:
  - Fear of parent falling when alone
  - Medication non-compliance
  - Lack of visibility into daily well-being
  - Coordination challenges among siblings

**2. Healthcare Providers (B2B)**
- **Target**: Primary care physicians, geriatricians
- **Size**: 220K primary care physicians in USA
- **Profile**:
  - Practices with ≥500 Medicare patients
  - Interest in value-based care models
  - Seeking new revenue streams (RPM reimbursement)
- **Pain Points**:
  - High hospital readmission rates
  - Medication non-adherence (40% of seniors)
  - Limited visibility between appointments
  - Time-intensive patient monitoring

**3. Senior Living Facilities (B2B)**
- **Target**: Independent/assisted living communities
- **Size**: 28,900 assisted living communities in USA
- **Profile**:
  - 100-300 resident communities
  - Premium positioning (high acuity residents)
  - Focused on safety and care quality
- **Pain Points**:
  - Staff shortages (can't monitor 24/7)
  - Liability concerns (falls, medication errors)
  - Family demands for transparency
  - Regulatory compliance (state oversight)

### Secondary Market Segments

**4. Medicare Advantage Plans**
- Partner with MA plans for at-risk populations
- Target: 28M Medicare Advantage enrollees

**5. Home Health Agencies**
- Supplement in-home nursing visits
- Target: 12K home health agencies

## Value Proposition

### For Families (B2C)
**"Peace of mind knowing your loved one is safe, even when you're miles away."**

**Key Benefits:**
- **Automatic fall detection** - No wearables to forget
- **Medication reminders** - Improve adherence without nagging
- **Family coordination** - Shared calendar, tasks, updates
- **Privacy-first** - No cameras, no audio recording
- **Affordable** - $29.99/month vs. $50+ for medical alert

**Emotional Appeal:**
- Reduce guilt and anxiety
- Enable aging in place (90% prefer)
- Strengthen family connections
- Avoid nursing home placement

### For Healthcare Providers (B2B)
**"Generate $10K-15K monthly in Medicare RPM reimbursement while improving patient outcomes."**

**Key Benefits:**
- **New revenue stream** - $150/patient/month (Medicare RPM)
- **Improved outcomes** - 30% reduction in readmissions
- **Time savings** - Automated data collection
- **Value-based care** - Meet quality metrics (MIPS)
- **Competitive advantage** - Differentiated care model

**ROI Example:**
- 100 enrolled patients × $150/month = $15K/month = $180K/year
- Platform cost: $50/patient/month × 100 = $5K/month = $60K/year
- Net revenue: $120K/year for 50 hours/month of nurse time

### For Senior Living Facilities (B2B)
**"Enhance safety, reduce liability, and improve family satisfaction with 24/7 monitoring."**

**Key Benefits:**
- **Reduce falls** - 40% reduction with early detection
- **Medication accuracy** - Eliminate manual errors
- **Staff efficiency** - Automated monitoring, alerts for action
- **Family transparency** - Real-time updates reduce complaints
- **Marketing advantage** - "Tech-enabled safety"

**ROI Example:**
- 200-bed facility
- Fall-related costs: $15K per fall × 50 falls/year = $750K
- Reduce falls 40% = $300K savings
- Platform cost: $20/resident/month × 200 = $48K/year
- Net savings: $252K/year + reduced liability + higher occupancy

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

### B2C Targets

**Paid Media CAC:**
- Google Ads: $80-100
- Facebook/Instagram: $60-80
- Average blended: $75

**Organic/Referral CAC:**
- SEO: $20 (long-term)
- Referral: $25 (incentive cost)

**Target LTV:CAC Ratio:** 3:1
- LTV = $30/month × 36 months × 70% retention = $756
- CAC target: <$250

### B2B Healthcare CAC

**Sales & Marketing CAC:**
- SDR + AE cost: $40K per AE (fully loaded)
- Quota: $700K ACV
- CAC: $40K / ($700K / $600 avg ACV) = ~$34 per patient

**Target LTV:CAC Ratio:** 5:1
- LTV = $50/month × 24 months = $1,200
- CAC target: <$240 per patient (covers sales cost)

### B2B Senior Living CAC

**Sales Cost:**
- AE + Implementation cost: $60K per AE
- Quota: 10 facilities × $40K = $400K ACV
- CAC: $60K / 10 = $6,000 per facility

**Target LTV:CAC Ratio:** 5:1
- LTV = 200 residents × $20/month × 24 months = $96K
- CAC target: <$20K per facility

## Customer Success & Retention

### Onboarding

**B2C:**
- Welcome email sequence (7 emails over 30 days)
- Video tutorials (setup, app walkthrough)
- Live chat support (Mon-Fri 9-5)
- 30-day check-in call (optional)

**B2B Healthcare:**
- Dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM)
- 90-day onboarding plan:
  - Week 1: Portal training, device distribution
  - Week 2-4: Enroll first 10 patients
  - Week 5-12: Scale to full quota, billing setup
- Monthly check-ins (first 6 months)
- Quarterly business reviews

**B2B Senior Living:**
- Dedicated Implementation Specialist
- 60-day implementation plan:
  - Week 1-2: Facility survey, WiFi assessment
  - Week 3-4: Device installation, staff training
  - Week 5-6: Resident enrollment, family communication
  - Week 7-8: Go-live, 24/7 monitoring active
- Weekly check-ins (first 3 months)
- On-site visits (quarterly)

### Support

**Channels:**
- Email: support@eldercare.plus (24-hour SLA)
- Phone: 1-800-ELDER-CARE (24/7 for emergencies)
- Live chat: Mon-Fri 9am-9pm ET
- Knowledge base: Self-service articles
- Community forum: Peer-to-peer support

**Support Metrics:**
- First response time: <2 hours (email), <30 seconds (phone)
- Resolution time: <24 hours (90%)
- CSAT score: >90%

### Retention Strategy

**Reduce Churn:**
- Identify at-risk customers (usage analytics)
- Proactive outreach (low engagement)
- Win-back campaigns (lapsed subscriptions)
- Exit surveys (understand churn reasons)

**Increase Engagement:**
- Monthly newsletters (tips, feature updates)
- Gamification (streaks, badges for medication adherence)
- Community features (family chat, shared photos)
- Telemedicine utilization (drive Premium upgrades)

**Expansion Revenue:**
- Upsell: Basic → Premium ($20/month increase)
- Cross-sell: Add second device, emergency button
- Referrals: Incentivize sibling/friend sign-ups

**Target Retention Rates:**
- B2C: 70% annual retention (30% churn)
- B2B Healthcare: 85% annual retention
- B2B Senior Living: 90% annual retention (long contracts)
